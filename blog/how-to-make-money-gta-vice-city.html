How to Make Money in GTA Vice City - Complete Guide

Published: September 14, 2026 · by QUENQQ Team · 12 min read

Money is everything in GTA Vice City. You need it to buy properties, unlock missions, purchase weapons, and build your criminal empire. This guide covers every method to make money fast, from quick street robberies in the early game all the way to earning $53,000 per day from passive property income.

All methods in this guide work in the browser version when you Play Quenq GTA Vice City Online Free on QUENQQ.

Quick Answer: The fastest early money is robbing all 15 stores (~$15,000 total) and running Vigilante missions on a police motorbike. Long term, buy properties as early as possible since they generate automatic daily income forever.

Method 1 - Rob Stores ~$1,000 per store

The fastest way to get cash at the start. Vice City has 15 robbable stores. Walk in, aim your weapon at the cashier, wait for them to empty the register, and grab the money. Each store pays around $1,000. Rob all 15 for an easy $15,000 with no missions required.

How to Rob a Store

  • Walk into any store with a cashier behind a counter
  • Pull out a weapon and aim at the cashier (right-click)
  • Hold your aim until they drop the money on the counter
  • Pick up the cash and leave before police arrive

Method 2 - Vigilante Missions $50 to $500+ per kill

Get into any police vehicle (car or motorbike) and press the mission button to start Vigilante. You chase and eliminate criminals. Each kill pays money, starting at $50 and increasing every consecutive kill. By kill 10 you earn $500 per target. By kill 20 you earn $1,000 per target.

  • Use the PCJ-600 motorbike for best speed and control
  • Use the Hunter helicopter (100 hidden packages reward) for maximum earnings
  • Complete 12 levels to permanently unlock 150 maximum armor
  • Earn $5,000 to $50,000+ in a single long session
Pro Tip: The Hunter military helicopter with missiles makes Vigilante trivial. You can earn over $500,000 in one session. Collect all 100 hidden packages to unlock it at your mansion.

Every story mission pays out on completion. Early missions pay $500 to $2,000. Mid-game missions pay $5,000 to $10,000. The Malibu Club bank heist pays $50,000 in a single mission.

This is the most important strategy in the entire game. After buying a property and completing its missions, it generates automatic daily income. Every time you walk into a property you own, you collect money that has been building up. This is how you become truly rich in Vice City.

Method 6 - Drug Dealer Farming $2,000 - $3,000 fast

Drug dealers in Vice City carry more cash than regular pedestrians. They spawn regularly in Little Haiti, near the docks, and around the Malibu Club area at night. Kill a dealer, collect the money, repeat. Use a silenced pistol to avoid attracting attention.

Warning: Killing people gives you wanted stars. Use a Pay and Spray after each run, or head to your safehouse to save and lose the stars for free.

Method 7 - Hidden Packages (Save Money on Weapons)

Hidden packages do not give cash directly but spawn free weapons at your safehouses. Normally weapons cost $1,500 to $10,000 each. Getting them free saves you enormous amounts of money over the whole game.

Step-by-Step Money Progression Plan

  1. Start: Rob all 15 stores. Earn around $15,000 total.
  2. Early: Do Pizza delivery to level 10 for $5,000 bonus. Run Vigilante on a motorbike to stack cash.
  3. First buy: Cherry Popper ($20,000). Sell to 50 customers for $3,000/day income.
  4. Second buy: Kaufman Cabs ($40,000). Three missions unlock $5,000/day.
  5. Keep going: Complete story missions to unlock the second island and better properties.
  6. Mid game: Print Works ($70,000), two missions, $8,000/day.
  7. Big investment: Malibu Club ($120,000). Bank heist pays $50,000 one time plus $10,000/day forever.
  8. Late game: Collect income daily from all properties. Earn $53,000+ per day automatically.

Money Mistakes to Avoid

  • Dying with cash: You lose 10% of your money on death. Save at a safehouse before risky missions.
  • Buying weapons before properties: Weapons are a one-time cost. Properties pay forever. Always prioritize properties.
  • Ignoring sub-missions: Permanent bonuses from sub-missions save you thousands in armor and health pickups throughout the game.
  • Not visiting properties: Income caps after a certain amount. Visit your properties regularly to collect and reset the income timer.
