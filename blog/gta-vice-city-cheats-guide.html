How to Enter Cheats in the Browser Version

Entering cheats in the browser version of GTA Vice City on QUENQQ is remarkably straightforward. Follow these simple steps for flawless activation:

  • Type during active gameplay: You do not need to pause the game, press ESC, or open an in-game console command prompt. Just type the cheat letters directly on your keyboard while Tommy is standing on foot or driving a car.
  • Case doesn't matter: You can type the codes in lowercase, uppercase, or mixed case (for example, typing aspirine or ASPIRINE works equally well).
  • Ignore Tommy's movements: Because certain letters match movement keys (such as 'W' for moving forward or 'A' for steering left), Tommy might take a few steps or turn while you type. This is completely normal and will not cancel the cheat code. Simply keep typing the letters in sequence.
  • Look for the confirmation alert: When entered correctly, a small confirmation message will appear in the top-left corner of the game screen displaying: "Cheat activated".
  • Make sure the game canvas is active: If you clicked outside the browser tab or clicked another window, click once inside the Vice City game screen to ensure keyboard focus is locked to the game before entering your code.
Pro Tip: To disable toggle cheats like slow motion (BOOOOOORING) or fast game speed (ONSPEED), simply retype the exact same cheat code a second time!

Critical Save Game Warnings: Cheats That Break Your Game

While health, weapon, weather, and vehicle cheats are completely safe to use during missions, pedestrian hostility cheats can permanently break your save file. Here is why you must exercise extreme caution:

⚠️ CRITICAL SAVE WARNING: Never save your game after activating FIGHTFIGHTFIGHT, NOBODYLIKESME, or OURGODGIVENRIGHTTOBEARARMS.

In GTA Vice City's game engine, pedestrian riot and aggression cheats permanently alter internal AI routing and hostility flags. Unlike weapon or money cheats that merely modify integer values in memory, the riot state is baked directly into the save file when you save at the Ocean View Hotel or the Vercetti Estate.

If you save your game after entering any of these three pedestrian cheats:

  • The cheat effect becomes permanent: Even if you close your browser, reload the save state, or reboot your computer, pedestrians will continue rioting, fighting each other, and attacking Tommy forever.
  • Storyline missions become impossible to beat: In many story missions (such as driving Mercedes to the Colonel's yacht or rescuing Lance Vance in "Death Row"), mission-critical NPCs will either be killed instantly by armed mobs or turn hostile and shoot Tommy, resulting in an immediate "Mission Failed" loop.
  • There is no reverse cheat code: There is no code to turn off pedestrian rioting once it has been saved to your file.
Golden Rule for Cheat Users: Always create a clean backup save in a separate save slot before testing chaos cheats! Once you are done having fun blowing up the city, simply reload your clean save file to continue your story progress safely.
