Quenq GTA Vice City Story: Vice City 1986

The story of GTA Vice City takes place in 1986 in a fictional city inspired by Miami, Florida. You play as Tommy Vercetti, a tough mobster who just spent 15 years in prison for the Forelli crime family.

The boss, Sonny Forelli, sends Tommy down to Vice City to start fresh and make money through a big drug deal. But the deal gets ambushed. Tommy loses the drugs and the money. Now Sonny is furious and wants every dollar back. Tommy has to figure out who set him up while building his own empire.

Along the way, Tommy meets some unforgettable characters:

  • Colonel Cortez : A retired military leader who runs shady operations from his luxury yacht
  • Ricardo Diaz : The biggest and most dangerous drug lord in Vice City
  • Lance Vance : Tommy's partner who wants to get revenge for his brother's murder
  • Avery Carrington : A wealthy real estate tycoon with dirty business tactics
  • Kent Paul : A British music manager who hangs out at the Malibu Club

As the story goes on, Tommy takes over Diaz's mansion and starts buying up businesses all over the city. From the Malibu Club to the Print Works, you build a criminal empire worth millions. The final battle is with Sonny Forelli himself, who shows up in Vice City to take everything from Tommy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Quenq Vice City free to play?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-ups, no downloads, and no hidden payments. You just open the page and play.

Does GTA Vice City online save my progress?

Yes. Your game is saved locally in your browser using IndexedDB. You get 8 save slots. Your saves will stay as long as you do not clear your browser data.

Can I play on my phone or tablet?

Yes. The game has full touch controls with on-screen buttons and dual joysticks. It works on both Android and iOS devices. For the best experience, use a device with at least 3 GB RAM.

What browser works best?

Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on desktop give the best performance. On mobile, Chrome for Android works great. Safari on iOS also works but may have slightly lower performance.

Do cheat codes work in the browser version?

Yes. All original GTA Vice City cheat codes work. Just type them on your keyboard during gameplay. A confirmation message appears in the top-left corner.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Connect any USB or Bluetooth gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation, or generic). The game will automatically detect it.

Why is the game running too fast?

GTA Vice City physics are tied to 30 FPS. If your monitor runs at 144Hz or higher, the game will speed up. Go to Options → Display Setup → enable Frame Limiter.
