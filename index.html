About GTA Vice City

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City was released by Rockstar Games in 2002 and became one of the best-selling games of all time. Set in a fictional version of 1986 Miami, you play as Tommy Vercetti, a mobster who gets sent to Vice City by the Forelli crime family to set up a drug deal. When the deal goes wrong, Tommy loses everything and has to fight his way through gang wars, corrupt cops, and rival drug lords to build his own criminal empire.

The game is known for its incredible 1980s atmosphere with neon lights, fast cars, palm trees, pastel suits, and an iconic licensed radio soundtrack featuring artists like Michael Jackson, Iron Maiden, Blondie, Hall & Oates, and Ozzy Osbourne.
